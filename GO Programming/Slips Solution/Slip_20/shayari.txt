Roz ki unhi raahon par
main har din ki tarah chal raha tha…
Par ek shaam kuch alag sa ho gaya…

Wahi bheed, wahi shor
wahi thaki hui si duniya…
Aur achanak saamne tu…
ek ajnabi si ladki
par dil ko bilkul apni si

Hum dono ek hi jagah baithe
na pehchaan… na koi naam…
Bas nazron ka ek halka sa salaam,
aur dil ka bechain sa aram…

Main kuch bolna chahta tha
par alfaaz ruk gaye…
Tu khamosh thi par teri khamoshi mein bhi
hazaar baatein chhupi lagti thi…

Do pal ka tha ye safar par khwaab hazaar ban gaye…
Subah ki pehli dhoop se lekar
raat ke aakhri lamhe tak bas tu hi tu…
mere khayalon mein reh gayi

Teri aankhon ka woh ek pal
mujhe andar tak chhoo gaya…
Jaise kisi anjaani si kahani mein
main khud ko pehchan gaya…

Phir achanak…
sawaari chal padi
Main dekhta reh gaya tu door hoti rahi…
Na main kuch keh paaya na tu kuch sun paayi…

Par jaate-jaate bhi tu mujhme kuch chhod gayi…
ek meethi si bechaini ek pyari si umeed…
aur ek adhuri si mulaqat

Raat bhar main usi pal ko yaadon mein dhundhta raha…
Aur meri dhadkano ne dheere se phus-phusakar kaha

Sun…
ye phir se
kisi ladki se pyaar ho raha…


 ~ Sanchet koleakr
